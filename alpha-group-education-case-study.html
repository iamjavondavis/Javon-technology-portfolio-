Alpha Group
Real Estate Education.

Turning complicated real estate information into a searchable, connected learning experience.

Information architectureUXResponsive interfacesAI-assisted development
Status: Design and responsive improvements saved in the website builder; public release and live interaction testing are not yet verified. No conversion or learning-impact metrics are claimed.
01 · Problem

Information without a clear path can overwhelm.

Real estate topics—financing, escrow, selling, ownership, taxes, liens, investing—are interconnected. Conventional landing pages can leave people searching across scattered articles and forms.

The design objective was to give visitors an intuitive way to begin with one question, explore related topics, and find the next useful resource.

02 · Experience model

Search, connect, explore.

Search intent→Knowledge map→Guide / topic→Optional next step

Conceptual diagram—not a screenshot or a verified live-user interaction.

03 · Documented work

A connected educational product.

  • Structured a search-first knowledge experience with 210 topics across 22 categories in the latest saved design.
  • Designed a premium, accessible-feeling information hierarchy that prioritizes learning over a hard sales pitch.
  • Connected educational topics to deeper explanations and resource pathways, with brokerage consultations positioned as optional next steps.
  • Refined responsive behavior so the mobile hero stacks and the navigation remains usable in narrower layouts.
  • Organized project handoff, scope boundaries, and offline checks in version-controlled documentation.
