01 · Business challenge

One business. Many disconnected journeys.

A real estate client may enter as a buyer, seller, investor, or general inquiry. Each path needs the right response, follow-up, education, ownership, and handling of consent—without conflicting automations or duplicate opportunities.

The project aims to standardize those journeys while preserving the distinct responsibilities of Javon Davis, Alpha Group Real Estate Education, Rescue My Residence, and DRIP AI.

03 · Implementation activities

Designing the logic—and its safeguards.

  • Mapped buyer, seller, investor and general inquiry paths and their different communications.
  • Configured and reviewed saved CRM workflows, forms, website drafts, and routing rules in GoHighLevel.
  • Designed personal-first acknowledgment followed by a separate education journey gated by permission checks.
  • Documented owner assignment, lead source preservation, DND/opt-out considerations, and duplicate-opportunity risks.
  • Maintained version-controlled specifications, change logs, test evidence, and unresolved release dependencies.

AI tools supported analysis, drafting, and implementation assistance. Human oversight and verification remained central.

05 · Professional capabilities

Why this matters to an employer.

This project demonstrates requirements gathering, operational process mapping, CRM configuration, stakeholder communication, change control, and disciplined launch preparation.

It also demonstrates the difference between building a configuration and proving a production outcome—an important distinction in enterprise implementations.

Impact measurement · Pending

Outcomes to measure after launch.

Lead-response latency, duplicate opportunity rate, correct owner assignment, consent-respecting education delivery, appointment conversion, and client handoff completeness. No revenue lifts or time savings are claimed without verified data.
